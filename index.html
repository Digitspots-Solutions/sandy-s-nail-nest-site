Frequently Asked Questions

How do I open a trading account?
Contact us via phone or WhatsApp. We'll guide you through the simple documentation process including identity verification, bank details, and CSCS registration. Account opening typically takes 3-5 business days.
What is the minimum investment amount?
You can start investing with as little as ₦10,000, though we recommend starting with ₦50,000 or more to allow for proper portfolio diversification.
What are your brokerage fees?
Our competitive brokerage commission is charged per transaction in accordance with NSE regulations. Contact us for our current fee schedule and any promotional rates for new clients.
How safe is my investment?
Jenny Stitches Stockbrokers Limited operates under the full regulation of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) of Nigeria. Your securities are held in your name at the Central Securities Clearing System (CSCS), ensuring maximum safety and transparency.
Can I access my portfolio online?
Yes, we provide regular portfolio statements and updates. Contact us to discuss online access options and mobile trading capabilities.
