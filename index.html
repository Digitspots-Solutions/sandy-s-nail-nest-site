About Us

At Sandy's Nail Nest, we believe every woman deserves to feel confident and beautiful. Located in the heart of Karu, Nasarawa State, we've built a reputation as the go-to destination for exceptional nail care and creative designs. Our passion is bringing your nail art dreams to life with precision, hygiene, and artistic flair. Whether you're preparing for a special occasion or simply treating yourself to some self-care, our skilled team ensures every visit leaves you feeling pampered and polished.

Our Services

Exceptional nail care tailored to your unique style

Classic Manicure & Pedicure

Treat your hands and feet to our signature spa treatment with nail shaping, cuticle care, exfoliation, massage, and polish. Perfect for maintaining healthy, beautiful nails.

Gel & Acrylic Nails

Long-lasting, durable nail enhancements that keep you looking fabulous for weeks. Choose from a variety of styles, lengths, and finishes.
